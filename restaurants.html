FAQ

Questions restaurants ask

Are these real client websites?

No. All six are self-initiated concept redesigns by UX CIRCLES, built to show how independent restaurants could present themselves online. Each concept site is clearly labelled as a concept and links to the restaurant's official website. We are not affiliated with these restaurants.

What does a UX CIRCLES restaurant website include?

A booking-first homepage, a readable menu (not a PDF), opening hours and location, private dining or catering enquiry pages where relevant, and optional Chinese or French versions. Sites are built as fast static pages that work well on phones.

Can you build a bilingual English and Chinese restaurant website?

Yes. Our team works in English and Traditional Chinese, and several concepts include Chinese-language versions. We can also add other languages, such as French, for restaurants with international guests.

Do you work with Taiwanese and Asian restaurants in the UK?

Yes. We are a London studio with Taiwanese roots, and half of these concepts are Taiwanese restaurants in London and Birmingham. We can brief and present in Mandarin.

Can my restaurant keep its existing booking system?

Usually, yes. A new website can link to or embed the booking tool you already use, so your team does not need to change how it takes reservations.

Book a free 25-minute call or email hello@ux-circles.com with your restaurant's name and current website. We will reply with an outline of what we would change and a quote.
