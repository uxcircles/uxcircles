FAQ

Common questions

常見問題

How much does a multilingual website for an accountant, law firm or estate agent cost?

UX CIRCLES builds multilingual professional services websites from £2,500. That covers up to five pages in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese, a regulatory disclosure section and schema markup. The domain and email are not included; hosting is part of the optional £99/month care plan.

What does it cost to run the website after launch?

Nothing is required: the site is yours. If you'd like us to look after it, the optional care plan is £99 a month or £990 a year. It covers hosting, SSL and uptime monitoring, up to 1 hour of updates a month across all languages, and a quarterly Google and AI search check. Extra work is £75 an hour and unused hours don't roll over. Your domain and email stay in your name, and if you cancel we hand over all site files.

Can I edit the website myself?

Your site is a static website with no admin panel, so there are no logins, plugins or security updates to manage, and it loads fast. Send changes by email and we update every language version for you, covered by the care plan or charged at £75 an hour. Content that changes often, such as fees, opening hours or team members, can be connected to a simple Google Sheet you update yourself.

Do you write Hong Kong or Taiwanese Chinese?

Both. We adapt Traditional Chinese vocabulary for Hong Kong or Taiwanese readers, and Simplified Chinese is included for mainland Chinese clients. For Hong Kong clients we also offer a separate version in colloquial written Cantonese as an add-on for £300.

Will my website meet SRA, OISC or estate agency rules?

We design a clear section for the information your regulator expects, such as SRA or OISC details, complaints procedures, price information, redress schemes or professional body membership. Your compliance lead confirms the final wording before launch; we are designers, not legal advisers.

How does a website help my firm appear in ChatGPT, Google AI or Perplexity answers?

AI assistants recommend firms whose websites state plainly what they do, where, in which languages and with which credentials. We add structured data for your firm and each adviser, write FAQ answers in English and Chinese, and keep your details consistent with directories such as Google Business Profile.

How long does it take?

A typical five-page multilingual site takes four to six weeks from brief to launch, depending on how quickly content and compliance details are approved.

會計師、律師或地產代理的中英多語網站要多少錢？

UX CIRCLES 的專業事務所多語網站 £2,500 起，包含最多 5 頁的英文、繁體中文、簡體中文版本、合規資訊區塊與結構化資料。不含網域與主機費用。

我可以自己修改網站嗎？

網站是沒有後台的靜態網站，不用管理帳號密碼、外掛或安全性更新，速度也快。需要修改時寄 email 給我們，我們會同步更新所有語言版本，費用由維護方案涵蓋或以每小時 £75 計費。經常變動的內容（例如收費、營業時間或團隊成員），可以連結到你自己就能修改的 Google 試算表。

可以用香港用語嗎？

可以。我們會依你的客群調整用語，例如香港讀者用「按揭」「物業」，台灣讀者用「房貸」「房產」，並同時提供簡體中文版。如需以粵語口語撰寫的廣東話版本，可加購 £300。

網站能讓 AI 推薦我的事務所嗎？

AI 助理會優先推薦網站資訊清楚的事務所：做什麼、在哪裡、講什麼語言、有什麼資格。我們會為事務所與每位專業人員加上結構化資料，並撰寫中英文常見問題，讓 AI 能直接引用。
