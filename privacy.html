01Who We Are

UX CIRCLES is an independent design studio based in the United Kingdom, providing brand identity, web design, and digital wedding invitation services.

For the purposes of UK GDPR, UX CIRCLES is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. You can contact us at hello@ux-circles.com.

02What Data We Collect

We only collect personal data when you actively provide it or when you visit our website:

  • Contact enquiries: If you email us or fill in a contact form, we receive your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Consultation bookings: If you book a consultation via cal.com, that platform collects your name, email, and booking details. Please refer to Cal.com's privacy policy for how they handle your data.
  • Project work: If you become a client, we may collect additional details necessary to complete your project (wedding details, photos, written content).
  • Analytics data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This data is aggregated and does not directly identify you. See section 4 for details.

03How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • To deliver the design services you have commissioned
  • To send project-related communications (drafts, feedback requests, invoices)
  • To improve our website using aggregated analytics

We will never use your data for unsolicited marketing without your explicit consent.

Our legal basis for processing your data is legitimate interests (responding to business enquiries and delivering contracted services) and, where applicable, contract performance.

04Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymised data about how visitors use our website — pages visited, time spent, and general geographic region. No personally identifiable information is included in this data.

GA4 sets cookies in your browser to distinguish visits. The main cookies used are:

  • _ga — distinguishes users, expires after 2 years
  • _ga_* — maintains session state, expires after 2 years

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party marketing tools on this website.

05Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share limited data with trusted third parties only where necessary to run our business:

  • Google Analytics — anonymised website usage data
  • Cal.com — booking details for consultation scheduling
  • Stripe — payment processing for invoices (if applicable); Stripe handles all card data and we never see your full card details

All third-party services used are GDPR-compliant. We do not transfer your data outside the UK or EEA without appropriate safeguards.

06How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Enquiries that don't become projects: Deleted after 12 months.
  • Client project data: Retained for up to 3 years after project completion for legitimate business purposes (invoicing records, portfolio reference), then securely deleted.
  • Analytics data: Retained for 14 months in Google Analytics (our default setting), after which it is automatically deleted.

07Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal obligations
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@ux-circles.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unsatisfied with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

08Contact

For any privacy-related questions or data requests:

  • Email: hello@ux-circles.com

We aim to respond to all data-related requests within 30 days, as required by UK GDPR.
