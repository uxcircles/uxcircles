Care plan · 維護方案

09Care plan: what's included維護方案內容

  • Hosting, SSL and uptime monitoring for your website.
  • Up to 1.5 hours of updates a month, across all the languages on your site.
  • A quarterly Google and AI search check, sent to you as a short report by email.
  • A full version history of your site, so any change can be undone.

Updates include text and image changes, adding or removing team members, small layout adjustments, and translating new content into your site's languages. New pages, redesigns and new features are not updates. We quote for them separately before starting.

  • 網站主機、SSL 憑證與上線監控。
  • 每月最多 1.5 小時的內容修改，涵蓋網站上的所有語言。
  • 每季一次 Google 與 AI 搜尋健檢，以簡短報告寄到你的信箱。
  • 保留完整的網站版本紀錄，任何修改都可以還原。

「修改」包含文字與圖片更新、新增或移除團隊成員、小幅版面調整，以及把新內容翻成網站上的各種語言。新增頁面、重新設計和新功能不算修改，我們會先另行報價。
