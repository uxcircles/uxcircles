Call Me Taiwan

台灣正名倡議 — 互動式倡議網站

A question most people have never had to ask: what if your country couldn't be called by its own name? Call Me Taiwan is an editorial-driven advocacy website designed to help international audiences understand — and feel — Taiwan's naming issue.

大多數人從未思考過：如果你的國家不能被叫做自己的名字，會是什麼感受？這個網站以互動與資料設計，讓國際觀眾真正理解台灣的正名處境。

The Challenge

Making an invisible injustice visible.

讓看不見的不公義，變得可見。

Taiwan is internationally known as "Chinese Taipei" or "Taiwan, Province of China" in some UN agencies and forms — names pushed by Beijing, never chosen by the Taiwanese people. The challenge wasn't just to inform, but to make people feel the absurdity of the situation.

如何讓一個從未親身經歷的問題，讓人感同身受？這是這個網站最核心的設計挑戰。

"What if the United States competed internationally as 'British Washington D.C.'?"

設計的核心策略：讓觀眾想像，如果換成自己的國家被改名，他們能接受嗎？

The design strategy: reframe the issue through empathy. Instead of presenting dry facts, we designed interactions that put the audience in Taiwan's shoes.

The project began as a tool for Taiwanese living abroad — a resource to help explain Taiwan to foreign friends. But the goal shifted: what if the site could make anyone feel it, without needing a Taiwanese person in the room to explain? That shift changed everything — the homepage had to work for a stranger, the interactions had to be self-evident, and the content had to be personalised to whoever was reading.

專案起初是為海外台灣人設計的工具，讓他們更容易向外國朋友解釋台灣。但目標後來轉移了：如果網站本身就能讓任何人感受到，不需要台灣人在旁邊解說呢？這個轉變改變了一切——首頁必須對陌生人有效，互動必須不言自明，內容必須針對每個閱讀者個人化。

Interactive Feature

Would you accept it?

如果換成你的國家，你能接受嗎？

A list asks you to read. A game asks you to feel. The mechanic: a Tinder-style card deck applying Taiwan's naming logic to other countries. The site detects the user's location and places their own nation's card first — the question becomes personal before it becomes political.

清單是讓人閱讀的，遊戲才是讓人感受的。Tinder 刷卡，每張牌把台灣的命名邏輯套用在另一個國家上。網站偵測用戶所在國，把他們自己國家的卡片排在最前面——問題在變成政治議題之前，先變成個人問題。

The final card is always Taiwan. After judging every other case, the answer is already in the user's hands. The closing line doesn't argue — it states: "Taiwan said the same NO. But the world doesn't care."

最後一張永遠是台灣。答案早已在用戶手上。結尾不是辯論，而是陳述：「台灣說了一樣的不。但世界不在乎。」

The "Would you accept it?" interactive — applying Taiwan's naming logic to other countries, personalised by location.

Data & Editorial Design

Numbers that tell a story.

用數據說一個讓人無法忽視的故事。

The site pairs editorial typography with carefully designed data visualisations — comparing Taiwan and China across press freedom, democracy index, GDP, and global recognition. Dark backgrounds and high-contrast type give the data emotional weight.

深色背景搭配高對比排版，讓台灣與中國的各項指標對比清晰可見，數字本身就在說話。

History Feature

A history worth knowing.

一段值得被認識的歷史。

The History section traces Taiwan's identity from its roots — from indigenous peoples through Dutch and Qing rule, Japanese colonisation, the arrival of the ROC, and the long road to democracy. The goal wasn't to present a political argument, but to show where Taiwan's sense of self actually comes from. A scroll-based timeline lets each era unfold as the reader moves through it.

歷史章節追溯台灣認同的根源——從原住民、荷據、清領、日治、ROC 遷台，到民主化的漫長過程。目的不是提出政治主張，而是讓人看見台灣的自我認同從何而來。以滾動式時間軸設計，讓每個時代隨著閱讀逐步展開。

The Record

Taiwan Renamed.

被改名的紀錄。

Since 1971, Taiwan has been forced to compete, participate, and exist under names it did not choose — by international bodies, corporations, and governments responding to Chinese pressure. This section documents the incidents: 31 recorded cases, and still growing.

自 1971 年起，台灣在奧運、聯合國機構、跨國企業、各國政府的壓力下，一次又一次被迫接受它從未選擇的名字。這個頁面記錄這些事件：目前 31 筆，持續增加中。

Taiwan Renamed — a documented record of forced renaming since 1971

Social Card Generator

Share the question.

把問題帶給更多人。

The final piece: a card generator that lets anyone create and download a shareable image — picking a country example, adding their own city, and choosing a format for Instagram, Twitter, or LinkedIn. The goal was to turn passive readers into active advocates, giving them a tool to start the conversation themselves.

最後一塊：社群卡片生成器，讓任何人都能選擇國家案例、輸入自己的城市，產出可分享的圖片。讓被動的閱讀者變成主動的倡議者——把問題帶給他們自己的社群。
