Care plan terms

網站維護方案服務條款

01Who these terms are for適用對象

These terms apply to the optional care plan for websites built by UX CIRCLES Ltd ("we", "us"). They sit alongside the quote or proposal for your website. If the two disagree, the signed quote wins.

本條款適用於由 UX CIRCLES Ltd 建置之網站所選購的維護方案，與網站報價單一併適用；如兩者內容不一致，以雙方確認的報價單為準。

02What's included方案內容

  • Hosting, SSL and uptime monitoring for your website.
  • Up to 1.5 hours of updates a month, across all the languages on your site.
  • A quarterly Google and AI search check, sent to you as a short report by email.
  • A full version history of your site, so any change can be undone.

Updates include text and image changes, adding or removing team members, small layout adjustments, and translating new content into your site's languages. New pages, redesigns and new features are not updates. We quote for them separately before starting.

  • 網站主機、SSL 憑證與上線監控。
  • 每月最多 1.5 小時的內容修改，涵蓋網站上的所有語言。
  • 每季一次 Google 與 AI 搜尋健檢，以簡短報告寄到你的信箱。
  • 保留完整的網站版本紀錄，任何修改都可以還原。

「修改」包含文字與圖片更新、新增或移除團隊成員、小幅版面調整，以及把新內容翻成網站上的各種語言。新增頁面、重新設計和新功能不算修改，我們會先另行報價。

03Price and payment費用與付款

  • Monthly: £99 a month, paid in advance.
  • Annual: £990 a year, paid in advance (the equivalent of two months free).
  • Prices are in GBP. Payment is by UK bank transfer or card via Stripe.
  • The plan starts on the day your website goes live.
  • We give at least 60 days' notice of any price change. An annual plan keeps its price until the end of the year you've paid for.
  • 月繳：每月 £99，預先支付。
  • 年繳：每年 £990，預先支付（等於送兩個月）。
  • 費用以英鎊計，可透過英國銀行轉帳或 Stripe 刷卡付款。
  • 方案自網站正式上線當天開始。
  • 如調整價格，會至少提前 60 天通知；已付款的年繳方案在該年度內維持原價。

04How update hours work修改時數如何計算

  • Send change requests by email to hello@ux-circles.com. We reply within 1 working day and usually complete small changes within 3 working days.
  • Time is logged in 15-minute blocks. You can ask for a log of the hours used at any time.
  • Hours reset at the start of each billing month. Unused hours don't roll over.
  • If a request would take you over your hours, we tell you first. Extra work is £75 an hour, billed in 15-minute blocks, and only with your approval.
  • 請以 email 寄送修改需求至 hello@ux-circles.com。我們會在 1 個工作天內回覆，小幅修改通常在 3 個工作天內完成。
  • 時數以每 15 分鐘為單位記錄，你可以隨時索取使用紀錄。
  • 時數於每個計費月開始時重新計算，未使用的時數不累積到下個月。
  • 如果某項需求會超出當月時數，我們會先告知你。超出部分以每小時 £75 計費，以 15 分鐘為單位，經你同意後才會進行。

05Hosting, domain and email主機、網域與 email

We host your site on a reliable global network and monitor it, but we can't guarantee it will never be unavailable. We aren't responsible for outages at third-party providers.

Your domain and email are not included and stay in your name. You keep paying your own domain registrar and email provider. To connect your site, you either point your domain's DNS to us or give us access to do it.

我們把網站放在穩定的全球網路上並持續監控，但無法保證網站永不中斷；第三方服務商發生的中斷，不在我們的責任範圍內。

網域與 email 不包含在方案內，並維持登記在你的名下，相關費用由你直接支付給網域與 email 服務商。連接網站時，由你把網域的 DNS 指向我們，或提供權限讓我們代為設定。

06Cancelling取消方案

  • Monthly plans: cancel any time with 30 days' notice by email.
  • Annual plans: cancel any time with 30 days' notice. We refund £990 minus £99 for each month used, counting the notice period. Once 10 or more months have been used, there is no refund.
  • When you cancel, we send you all your website files within 10 working days and help you move to the host of your choice.
  • We may end the plan with 60 days' notice. If we do, we refund any months already paid for and not yet used.
  • 月繳方案：隨時可以 email 通知取消，需提前 30 天。
  • 年繳方案：隨時可以取消，需提前 30 天通知。退款金額為 £990 減去已使用月數 × £99（通知期也算在已使用月數內）；已使用 10 個月以上則不退款。
  • 取消時，我們會在 10 個工作天內交出全部網站檔案，並協助你搬到你指定的主機。
  • 我們也可以提前 60 天通知終止方案，並退還已付款但尚未使用的月份。

07Late payment逾期付款

If a payment is more than 14 days late, we may pause updates until it's paid. If it's more than 30 days late, we may suspend hosting after giving you 7 days' written notice. We'll always try to sort it out with you first.

如付款逾期超過 14 天，我們可能暫停內容修改直到款項付清；逾期超過 30 天，我們可能在書面通知 7 天後暫停主機服務。我們一定會先與你聯絡處理。
