FAQ

Fair questions

常見問題

Why is it free?

We'd rather show you than pitch you. A finished homepage design says more about how we work than any proposal. If you like it, we can talk about building it. If not, keep the design. We won't chase you.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. The design is yours to keep and use however you like. There's no contract and no obligation to work with us.

What do you need from me?

Your web address and a few answers about your business, which takes about two minutes. We work from your site as your clients see it, so there are no logins to share.

How do you choose the 5 businesses each month?

We pick businesses where a new homepage would make the biggest difference, and where we have room to do it properly. If you're not picked this month, we'll tell you and can hold your place for the next one.

Will you show my redesign publicly?

Only if you say so. The form asks whether you're happy for us to share it, with a credit and link to your site, or without your name and logo. The default is private.

What does the AI search check look at?

Whether Google, ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can find your business, describe it correctly and recommend it for the services you offer, plus the three fixes that would help most. You get it as a short report by email.
